## 1. The plans

Badna has three plans. Free is free for as long as you like. Pro and Household are subscriptions.

| | Free | Pro | Household |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lists you own | 2 | No limit | No limit |
| People on each list you own, you included | 2 | 2 | 6 |
| New items on the lists you own, counting what every member adds | 200 in any 30 days | No limit | No limit |
| Lists other people share with you | No limit | No limit | No limit |
| Apple Family Sharing, on iPhone and iPad | Not applicable | No | Yes |

Three rules hold for every plan:

- **The list owner's plan sets the limits on a list.** When you add to a list someone else owns, their plan decides how many people the list can have and how many items it takes, and your own plan is not used up by it. When other people add to a list you own, your plan is the one that counts.
- **Seats include you.** "6 people" on Household means you and five others on each list you own. No plan offers more than six people on a list, and no plan offers an unlimited number of people.
- **Nothing about a plan is hidden in the app.** The limits that apply to you are shown in the app, the app tells you when you reach one, and the paywall shows what each plan includes before you buy.

We may change what a plan includes. We never lower a limit on a paid plan you are subscribed to without telling you in the app at least 30 days beforehand, so that you can cancel before the change takes effect; a change that raises a limit or adds something may take effect at once. The Free plan's limits are in the terms of service, section 5.

## 2. Prices

Prices are set per country and shown by the App Store or Google Play, in your currency, before you confirm a purchase. The price on the paywall is the price the store will charge you for the first paid period and, unless we change the price, for every renewal. Where the store adds tax, the store shows it; the store may also show a price that includes tax where the law requires. We never see your card number or billing address.

Each plan is sold monthly or yearly. The yearly price is lower than twelve months of the monthly price, and the paywall shows the saving and the yearly price spread over a month next to it, so you can compare. The amount billed is always the full price of the period.

If we raise the price of a plan, the store tells you before the new price applies to you, and in the cases where the store's rules require your agreement, the price does not change for you until you agree. Where the store does not need your agreement, you still get notice in time to cancel before the new price is charged. If we lower a price, your next renewal is charged at the lower price.

The two yearly plans start with a free trial of 7 days for new subscribers; the monthly plans have none. Before you start a trial, the store shows how long it lasts, what the plan costs when it ends and that it turns into a paid subscription unless you cancel before the end, and the paywall says the same. Nothing is charged when you start a trial. When it ends, the store charges the yearly price and the subscription renews as section 3 says, unless you cancel before the trial ends; on the App Store, turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before it ends. During the trial you have the plan's full limits, and section 4 says how to cancel. The store decides whether you qualify: the trial is for new subscribers, and the store does not give you a second one. The Free plan has no time limit and lets two people use the whole app for as long as you like. If we offer another trial or a discount later, the store shows its terms before you start it.

## 3. Payment and renewal

Payment is charged to your Apple Account or your Google Play account when you confirm the purchase, or, if you start with a free trial, when the trial ends. The subscription renews automatically, for the same period and at the same price, until you cancel it:

- **App Store.** Your account is charged for the next period within 24 hours before the current period ends. To stop a renewal, turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the current period ends.
- **Google Play.** Your account is charged for the next period when the current one ends. To stop a renewal, cancel before the current period ends.

The store sends you a receipt for every charge, and tells you before a price change as section 2 says. Some stores also remind you before a yearly renewal; we do not control when or whether they do.

If a renewal payment fails, the store tries again and asks you to update your payment method. During the grace period the store gives for that, you keep your plan. If the payment is not recovered by the end of the grace period, the subscription ends and section 7 applies. If you then fix the payment method, the store resumes the subscription and your plan comes back.

Your plan is set by the subscription the store confirms to us. After a purchase, renewal, change or cancellation, the store tells us and the app shows the new plan within about a minute. If it does not, open the paywall and tap "Restore purchases", or write to us.

## 4. Cancelling

You cancel in the store you bought the plan from, not in the app, and we cannot cancel it for you:

- **iPhone or iPad.** Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Badna and tap Cancel Subscription.
- **Android.** Open the Google Play app, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, choose Badna and tap Cancel subscription.

"Manage subscription" in the app, on the Profile screen and on the paywall, opens that page of the store for you.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep your plan until the end of the period you have already paid for, and nothing is charged after that. If you cancel during a free trial, you keep the plan until the trial ends and nothing is charged; on the App Store, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Cancelling does not refund the current period; section 5 says when a refund is possible.

Deleting the app from your device does not cancel a subscription. Deleting your Badna account does not cancel it either, because the subscription is between you and the store: cancel it in the store first, then delete the account. The delete-account screen reminds you of this and links to the store's subscription page.

## 5. Refunds

Apple and Google take your payment, so they decide refunds, under their own rules and the consumer law of the country you bought in. We cannot refund a store purchase ourselves, and we cannot see your payment method.

- **App Store.** Ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, or through the App Store app. Apple answers most requests within 48 hours and decides whether to refund.
- **Google Play.** Ask Google through the Google Play app or at play.google.com under Order history. Google may also point you to us to confirm a problem with the app, and we will help with that.

If you live in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to withdraw from a digital service for 14 days after you buy it, and the stores honour that right for a subscription you have just started. After those 14 days, the stores refund only where the service was faulty or not as described, or where your local consumer law requires it. Where you live elsewhere, the store's refund rules for your country apply. If you believe the app did not do what this page says it does, write to us at hello@antarsoft.dev as well, so that we can fix the problem and support your request with the store.

When the store refunds a subscription, or reverses a charge, it tells us and your plan returns to Free, usually within a minute; section 7 says what that means for your lists. Nothing is deleted.

## 6. Changing plans

You hold one plan at a time on one store account. On the paywall, the button says what a change will do before you confirm it:

- **Pro to Household.** The upgrade takes effect at once, and six seats apply to your lists as soon as the store confirms it. The store charges you the difference for the time left in your current period: on the App Store you are charged for Household and refunded the unused part of Pro; on Google Play the price of Household is reduced by the unused part of Pro, or, when you move from Pro monthly to Household yearly, the unused Pro time is credited to you as time on Household, because Google Play cannot charge a difference when the price per month goes down.
- **Household to Pro.** The downgrade takes effect at your next renewal date. You keep Household until then, and Pro is charged from that date. From then on each list you own can have two people, and section 7 says how you choose who stays.
- **Monthly to yearly, or yearly to monthly, on the same plan.** Your limits do not change. On the App Store the new period starts at your next renewal date. On Google Play the switch is immediate and the new price is charged on your next billing date.

The store, not the app, works out any amount owed or credited for a change, and its receipt shows it. If you want to move from a plan bought on one store to the other store, cancel the first subscription and let it end before you buy on the second; the app cannot merge two subscriptions, and a second active subscription on another store account is not refunded by us.

## 7. When a paid plan ends

A paid plan ends when you cancel and the paid period or the free trial runs out, when a renewal fails and the store's grace period passes, when the store refunds or reverses a purchase, or when a Household plan becomes Pro at renewal. Your plan then becomes Free, or Pro, and the limits of that plan apply to the lists you own. Nothing is deleted when that happens.

**If your lists are over the new limits**, the app asks you to choose before you use them again. You pick which lists stay active, up to the new plan's limit, and who stays on any list that has more people than the new plan allows. Nobody is removed and no list is made inactive until you save your choices. Until you save them, the app shows you that choice screen instead of your lists, and you can also upgrade again from there.

**Inactive lists keep everything on them.** You can see an inactive list, but nobody can add to it, and the other people on it can see it but cannot open it. You can make it active again when you have room under your plan, or by moving to a paid plan.

**People you remove** lose access to that list at once. What they added stays on the list, because it is part of the list.

**Members of your lists keep their own plans.** When your plan ends, the people on your lists lose only what your plan gave them on your lists: their own lists stay on their own plan, with their own limits, and nothing they own changes.

If you had Household and move to Pro, the same choice applies to lists with more than two people on them, and your number of lists and items stays unlimited.

## 8. Shared lists and members

You never pay for a list someone else owns, and nobody pays for yours. When you are a member of a list, the owner's plan decides how many people can be on it and how many items it takes; when a list reaches a limit, every member sees a message saying so, which names the plan that set the limit. Buying Pro or Household lifts the limits on the lists you own, not on lists other people own, and a member on your Household list does not get Household for their own lists unless Family Sharing gives it to them, as section 9 says.

Other members of your lists do not see your receipts or your store account, and they learn which plan a list is on only when the list reaches one of its limits. The owner of a list sees the limits that apply to it and nothing about the members' plans.

## 9. Family Sharing

On iPhone and iPad, Household can be shared with your Apple Family Sharing group. Up to five family members receive Household on their own accounts, so their own lists also get Household limits; on the lists you own they are members like anyone else. Pro cannot be shared. Family Sharing is set up and controlled in your Apple Account settings, and it ends for a family member when they leave your family group, when you stop sharing the subscription, or when the subscription ends; their plan then returns to their own, and section 7 applies to the lists they own. Google Play has no Family Sharing for subscriptions, so on Android a Household covers the lists its subscriber owns.

## 10. Store accounts, restoring and devices

Your subscription belongs to the store account you bought it with, and the app attaches it to the Badna account you were signed in to when you bought it. On a new device, or after reinstalling, sign in and tap "Restore purchases" on the paywall or the Profile screen; the store confirms the subscription and your plan comes back. A plan bought on the iPhone or Android app also applies when you use the web version. Subscriptions are sold only in the iPhone and Android apps.

One store subscription works for one Badna account at a time, and the store does not sell you the same subscription twice. If you subscribe, or restore, with a store account whose subscription is already attached to a different Badna account, the app tells you; sign in with that account, or write to us. If you delete your account and later sign up again with the same store account, "Restore purchases" attaches the still-running subscription to the new account; nothing else comes back, as the deletion page at `[DELETION_URL]` says.
