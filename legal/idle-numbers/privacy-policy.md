# Privacy Policy — Idle Numbers

**Last updated:** 2026-08-09  
**Developer:** Antarsoft (`com.antarsoft.idlenumbers`)  
**Contact:** Discord — https://discord.gg/zcZTAQq

This policy describes what Idle Numbers collects and how that data is used on iOS.

## What the game stores on your device

Progress, settings, and purchase entitlements (for example Remove Ads) are saved locally
on your device (PlayerPrefs / app storage). That data stays on the device unless you
reset progress or delete the app.

## Advertising

If you have not purchased Remove Ads, the game may show rewarded and interstitial ads
through **Unity LevelPlay / ironSource** and its mediation partners. Those networks may
collect device identifiers and usage data for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and
measurement, subject to their own privacy policies and your iOS tracking choices
(App Tracking Transparency when shown).

Purchasing **Remove Ads** turns off ads in the app. You can restore that purchase from
Settings → Restore Purchases.

## In-app purchases

Purchases go through the **Apple App Store**. We receive purchase / restore status so
we can unlock Remove Ads. Apple processes payment details under Apple’s privacy policy;
we do not receive your full payment card information.

## Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple may associate leaderboard / achievement activity
with your Game Center account under Apple’s terms.

## Analytics and diagnostics

The build may use platform crash / diagnostics tooling (for example Unity Cloud
Diagnostics) so we can fix bugs. Those reports can include device model, OS version,
and crash stacks. They are not used to sell advertising profiles.

## Children

Idle Numbers is a general-audience idle game. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children for marketing. Parental controls and iOS Screen Time apply
as configured on the device.

## Your choices

- Disable ads by purchasing Remove Ads (or Restore Purchases if you already own it).
- Limit ad tracking via iOS Settings / ATT prompts.
- Reset local progress from Settings (does not refund App Store purchases).
- Delete the app to remove local save data on the device.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the product or SDKs change. The “Last updated” date
above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the
revised policy.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: join Discord (link above) and open a support thread.
