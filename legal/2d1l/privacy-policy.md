# Privacy Policy

**Two Dots One Line**  
**Antarsoft**  
**Effective date:** November 9, 2026

---

## Overview

Two Dots One Line is a single-player arcade game for iPhone, iPad and Android. Most of the game works offline, but two optional features — online leaderboards (Apple Game Center on iOS, Google Play Games on Android) and rewarded video ads — require an internet connection and involve Apple, Google and our advertising partner. This policy explains what the app stores on your device, what may be shared with others, and the choices you have.

---

## Information We Collect

### What is stored on your device

The app saves information locally on your device. This includes:

- Your high scores for each difficulty (Easy, Normal, Expert)
- Your selected difficulty and in-game settings (such as background quality and title style)
- Whether you have unlocked higher difficulties based on your scores

This information stays on your device. We do not run game servers or cloud saves, and we do not receive your save data.

### What we do not collect ourselves

We do not directly collect or access:

- Your name, email address, Apple ID or Google account details (Apple or Google may share limited leaderboard profile information with the app — see below)
- Your precise location
- Your camera or photos
- Your microphone or voice recordings
- Your contacts, calendar, or address book
- Health or fitness data

We do not use any analytics SDK of our own. Our advertising partner may collect some device information, including approximate location derived from your IP address, to deliver and measure ads — see **Advertising** below.

## Leaderboards

Two Dots One Line offers optional online leaderboards for each difficulty.

### Apple Game Center (iPhone and iPad)

- If you choose to sign in to Game Center, Apple provides the app with your Game Center player identity (such as your nickname) so your scores can be displayed on the leaderboards.
- This feature is handled by Apple. We do not receive or store your Apple ID, email address, or real name. Apple's collection and use of your information is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### Google Play Games (Android)

- If you sign in to Google Play Games, Google provides the app with your Play Games player profile (such as your gamer name and avatar) so your scores can be displayed on the leaderboards.
- This feature is handled by Google. We do not receive or store your Google account password or email address. Google's collection and use of your information is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can manage your Play Games profile and visibility in the Google Play Games app.

### On both platforms

- Leaderboard scores you submit (your high score and player identity) may be visible to other players who view that leaderboard.
- You can decline to sign in. The game is fully playable without it — you simply won't see or submit leaderboard scores.

## Advertising

Two Dots One Line offers optional rewarded video ads (for example, to continue after a game over) on iOS and Android using **Unity LevelPlay** (ironSource) ad mediation, including Unity Ads.

- Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward.
- To deliver, measure, and mediate ads, and to prevent fraud, Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with may collect device information such as device and advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, interactions with ads, and app diagnostics, in accordance with their own privacy policies.
- On iOS, if any ad partner wants to track you across apps and websites, Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework requires them to ask for your permission first. You can allow or deny this at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.
- On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in **Settings → Google → Ads** (or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**, depending on your device).
- You can see Unity's advertising and privacy practices at [Unity's Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## Data Retention and Deletion

- Your save data (scores and settings) remains on your device until you delete it (for example, through **Reset score** in the app menu) or until you uninstall the app.
- Your Game Center data is managed by Apple, and your Google Play Games data is managed by Google. You can control or delete it through your Apple ID or Google account settings.
- Uninstalling the app removes your local save data from your device.

### How to request deletion of your data

We do not hold personal data on our own servers. To request that data associated with your use of Two Dots One Line is deleted, including data collected by our advertising partner, email **hello@antarsoft.com** with the subject "Data deletion – Two Dots One Line". If you can, include your device's advertising ID so we can pass the request to our advertising partner. We will respond within 30 days.

## Your Choices

- You can reset your scores at any time from the in-game menu.
- You can play the game fully offline without signing in to Game Center or Google Play Games.
- You can choose not to watch rewarded ads — they are entirely optional.
- You can allow or deny ad tracking (iOS) or reset and opt out of your advertising ID (Android) at any time in your device settings.
- You can choose not to open external links to Discord or other websites.

---

## Security

Your game data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload your save files to our servers. Data sent by the app's third-party services (Apple, Google and Unity LevelPlay) is encrypted in transit.
