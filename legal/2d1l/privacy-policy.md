# Privacy Policy

**2D1L**  
**Antarsoft**  
**Effective date:** September 3, 2026

---

## Overview

2D1L is a single-player arcade game. Most of the game works offline, but two optional features — Game Center leaderboards and rewarded video ads — require an internet connection and involve Apple and our advertising partner. This policy explains what the app stores on your device, what may be shared with others, and the choices you have.

---

## Information We Collect

### What is stored on your device

The app saves information locally on your iPhone or iPad. This includes:

- Your high scores for each difficulty (Easy, Normal, Expert)
- Your selected difficulty and in-game settings (such as background quality and title style)
- Whether you have unlocked higher difficulties based on your scores

This information stays on your device. We do not run game servers or cloud saves, and we do not receive your save data.

### What we do not collect ourselves

We do not directly collect or access:

- Your name, email address, or Apple ID (Apple may share limited Game Center information with the app — see below)
- Your location
- Your camera or photos
- Your microphone or voice recordings
- Your contacts, calendar, or address book
- Health or fitness data

We do not use any analytics SDK of our own. Our advertising partner may collect some device information to deliver and measure ads — see **Advertising** below.

---

## Game Center

2D1L uses Apple's **Game Center** to offer optional leaderboards for each difficulty.

- If you choose to sign in to Game Center, Apple provides the app with your Game Center player identity (such as your nickname) so your scores can be displayed on the leaderboards.
- Leaderboard scores you submit (your high score and Game Center identity) may be visible to other players who view that leaderboard.
- This feature is entirely handled by Apple. We do not receive or store your Apple ID, email address, or real name. Apple's collection and use of your information is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- You can decline to sign in to Game Center. The game is fully playable without it — you simply won't see or submit leaderboard scores.

---

## Advertising

2D1L offers optional rewarded video ads (for example, to continue after a game over) using **Unity LevelPlay** (ironSource) ad mediation.

- Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward.
- To deliver, measure, and mediate ads, Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with may collect device information (such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, and general app usage) in accordance with their own privacy policies.
- On iOS, if any ad partner wants to track you across apps and websites, Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework requires them to ask for your permission first. You can allow or deny this at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking** on your device.
- You can see Unity's advertising and privacy practices at [Unity's Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy).
- Currently, 2D1L's advertising is enabled on iOS only.

## In-App Purchases

2D1L does not currently offer any in-app purchases. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated before any purchase feature is released.

## Third-Party Links

If you choose to tap certain buttons in the app, you may be taken to external websites in your browser, such as our Discord community or a support page. Those websites have their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

---

## Children's Privacy

2D1L is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

---

## Data Retention and Deletion

- Your save data (scores and settings) remains on your device until you delete it (for example, through **Reset score** in the app menu) or until you uninstall the app.
- Your Game Center data is managed by Apple and can be controlled through your Apple ID and device settings.
- Uninstalling the app removes your local save data from your device.

---

## Your Choices

- You can reset your scores at any time from the in-game menu.
- You can play the game fully offline without signing in to Game Center.
- You can choose not to watch rewarded ads — they are entirely optional.
- You can allow or deny ad tracking permissions at any time in your device settings.
- You can choose not to open external links to Discord or other websites.

---

## Security

Your game data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload your save files to our servers. Game Center features are protected by Apple's standard security, and advertising is delivered through Unity LevelPlay's standard security practices.
