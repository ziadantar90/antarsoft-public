# Privacy Policy

**Ada and Cal**  
**Antarsoft**  
**Effective date:** September 19, 2026

---

## Overview

Ada and Cal is a single-player puzzle game. Most gameplay works offline. We collect very little information. This policy explains what the app stores on your device, what may be shared with Apple services, and the choices you have.

---

## Information We Collect

### What is stored on your device

The app saves information locally on your iPhone or iPad. This includes:

- Your level progress and completion status
- Your best move counts per level (used for in-game progress and leaderboard scores)
- In-game settings (such as graphics quality)
- Whether you have unlocked the full game after a purchase
- Small flags used for in-app prompts (for example, rating reminders after certain levels)

This information stays on your device. We do not run game servers or cloud saves, and we do not receive your save files.

### What we do not collect

The app does not collect or access:

- Your name, email address, or Apple ID (except where Apple handles purchases or Game Center — see below)
- Your location
- Your camera or photos
- Your microphone or voice recordings
- Your contacts, calendar, or address book
- Health or fitness data
- Advertising or tracking data for third-party ad networks
- Analytics or marketing profiles

---

## In-App Purchases

You can play a free demo that includes the tutorial and the opening levels of Chapter 1. To access the rest of the game, you may purchase a one-time unlock for the full game.

- All payments are handled by Apple through the App Store.
- We do not receive or store your payment card details.
- Apple may send us confirmation that a purchase was completed so the app can unlock the full game on your device.
- Product ID: `com.antarsoft.adaandcal.fullgame`
- If you reinstall the app or switch devices, you can use **Restore Purchases** in the app to restore your unlock on the same Apple ID.

---

## Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center (or if you are signed in through iOS), the app may submit leaderboard scores (such as move counts for levels and a total-moves leaderboard) to Apple. Apple processes and displays that activity under [Apple’s privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and Game Center terms. We do not receive your Apple ID or email from Game Center; we only receive success or failure when submitting scores.

You can avoid leaderboard submission by not signing in to Game Center or by not using leaderboard features in the app.

## Third-Party Links

If you choose to tap certain buttons in the app, you may be taken to external websites in your browser. These may include:

- **Discord community:** [https://discord.gg/zcZTAQq](https://discord.gg/zcZTAQq)
- **Antarsoft website:** [http://www.antarsoftgames.com](http://www.antarsoftgames.com)
- Music credits (for example, a YouTube channel link in the credits screen)
- This privacy policy, when linked from the app or App Store listing

Those websites and services have their own privacy policies, which we do not control. Discord’s privacy policy applies when you visit or use our Discord server.

---

## Children's Privacy

Ada and Cal is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

---

## Data Retention and Deletion

- Your save data remains on your device until you delete it (for example, through **Reset Progress** in the app) or until you uninstall the app.
- Your purchase unlock is stored on your device and can be restored through Apple if you reinstall the app on the same Apple ID.
- Uninstalling the app removes your local save data from your device.
- Leaderboard data already submitted to Game Center is managed by Apple according to your Apple ID settings.

---

## Your Choices

- You can reset local progress from the in-game menu.
- You can play the free demo without making a purchase.
- You can choose not to open external links to Discord, our website, or other sites.
- You can limit Game Center activity through your device’s Game Center and Apple ID settings.

---

## Security

Your game data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload your save files to our servers. In-app purchases are protected by Apple’s standard App Store security.
